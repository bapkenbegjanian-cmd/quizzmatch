Sample questions

  1. Which country has a white flag with a red disc in the middle?
    Japan · China · South Korea · Vietnam
    Show the answerJapan : The disc stands for the rising sun.
  2. Which country has a square red flag with a white cross in the middle?
    Switzerland · Denmark · England · Sweden
    Show the answerSwitzerland : It's one of only two square national flags, with the Vatican's.
  3. Which country has a green flag with a yellow diamond and a blue globe?
    Brazil · Portugal · Mexico · Jamaica
    Show the answerBrazil : The globe carries the motto "Ordem e Progresso".
  4. Which animal is on the flag of Wales?
    A red dragon · A lion · An eagle · A unicorn
    Show the answerA red dragon : Y Ddraig Goch, the red dragon.
  5. Which country has a red flag with a white crescent and star?
    Turkey · Pakistan · Algeria · Tunisia
    Show the answerTurkey : Tunisia's flag also has a crescent, but inside a white disc.
  6. Which country has a green and white flag with a red crescent and star?
    Algeria · Mauritania · Pakistan · Libya
    Show the answerAlgeria : Green on the left, white on the right.
  7. Which country has a red flag with a white Nordic cross?
    Denmark · Switzerland · Norway · England
    Show the answerDenmark : The Dannebrog, one of the oldest flags in the world.
  8. Which country has a red flag with a black double-headed eagle?
    Albania · Serbia · Montenegro · Germany
    Show the answerAlbania : The double-headed eagle is the national symbol.
  9. What are the colours of the Irish flag, starting from the pole?
    Green, white, orange · Green, white, red · Orange, white, green · Green, yellow, orange
    Show the answerGreen, white, orange : White stands for peace between the two communities.
  10. Which is the only national flag that is neither square nor rectangular?
    Nepal's · Bhutan's · The Vatican's · Switzerland's
    Show the answerNepal's : It's made of two stacked triangles.
  11. Which country has a cedar tree on its flag?
    Lebanon · Cyprus · Jordan · Canada
    Show the answerLebanon : A green cedar between two red stripes.
  12. Which country's flag is almost identical to Indonesia's?
    Monaco · Poland · Singapore · Austria
    Show the answerMonaco : Red on top, white below; only the proportions differ.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "World flags" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "World flags" quiz?

37 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
