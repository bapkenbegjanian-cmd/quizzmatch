About QuizzMatch

QuizzMatch is an independent site of free quizzes, built to be played with friends, on a phone or a computer.

What the site offers

The site currently has 27 quizzes and over 2,000 questions in categories such as general knowledge, history, geography, science, sport, film, TV series, music, manga, languages and personality tests. Every quiz has three levels (easy, medium, hard) and every game draws 10 questions at random.

After each answer, a short explanation gives the context: the goal is to learn something, not just to score points.

Playing with friends

At the end of a game you can send a challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode lets you play live, each on your own phone. A leaderboard for each level keeps the best scores of every quiz.

How the questions are written

Questions are written by hand, then reviewed and checked before going live: one right answer, plausible wrong answers, a short explanation. Questions that only make sense to a French audience are offered in French only. If you spot a mistake, let us know and it will be fixed quickly.

Free, no sign-up

No account is needed. The site is funded by advertising and by donations from players who want to support it. To learn about data, read the privacy policy.
