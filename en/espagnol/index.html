    Show the answerPlease : « De nada » means you're welcome.

    Cat · Cake · Dog · Duck
    Show the answerCat : A cake is « pastel ».

    Red · Pink · Yellow · Black
    Show the answerRed : Yellow is « amarillo ».
  5. What does « tres » mean?
    Three · Very · Thirteen · Thirty

    Apple · Mango · Hand · Morning

  10. What does « grande » mean?
    Big · Fat · Seed · Attic

Frequently asked questions

Is the "Spanish" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "Spanish" quiz?

89 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
