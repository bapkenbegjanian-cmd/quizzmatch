Sample questions

  1. In France, what is the motorway speed limit in dry weather, with more than 3 years of licence?
    110 km/h · 120 km/h · 130 km/h · 150 km/h
    Show the answer130 km/h : It drops to 110 km/h in the rain.
  2. On the motorway, a new driver is limited to:
    100 km/h · 110 km/h · 120 km/h · 130 km/h
    Show the answer110 km/h : During the probationary period.
  3. Steady amber light: what should you do?
    Speed up to get through · Stop, unless it's dangerous · Honk · Go if nobody is coming
    Show the answerStop, unless it's dangerous : Amber means stop, unless you can't safely.
  4. At a stop sign, you must:
    Slow right down · Come to a full stop at the line · Stop only if a car is coming · Honk
    Show the answerCome to a full stop at the line : A full stop is required, even on an empty road.
  5. Is the seat belt compulsory in the back?
    Yes, always · No, never · Only on the motorway · Only at night
    Show the answerYes, always : Compulsory in the back since 1990.
  6. How many points does a probationary licence start with?
    6 · 12 · 8 · 3
    Show the answer6 : It goes up to 12 after the probationary period without offences.
  7. Maximum blood alcohol level for a full licence?
    0.5 g per litre of blood · 0.2 g per litre of blood · 0.8 g per litre of blood · 1 g per litre of blood
    Show the answer0.5 g per litre of blood : That's 0.25 mg per litre of exhaled air.
  8. Which equipment is compulsory in the car?
    A hi-vis vest and a warning triangle · A fire extinguisher · A first-aid kit · A spare wheel
    Show the answerA hi-vis vest and a warning triangle : The vest must be reachable from the seat.
  9. What does a yellow diamond sign mean?
    Priority road · Danger · End of road · Roundabout
    Show the answerPriority road : You have priority at junctions.
  10. At what age can you take the car licence in France since 2024?
    17 · 16 · 18 · 15
    Show the answer17 : You can drive alone from 17.
  11. What is the speed limit in a shared zone ("zone de rencontre")?
    20 km/h · 10 km/h · 30 km/h · 50 km/h
    Show the answer20 km/h : Pedestrians have priority there.
  12. Can you honk in town?
    Only in case of immediate danger · Always · To say hello · Only at night
    Show the answerOnly in case of immediate danger : Otherwise it's an offence.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "French driving test" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.
