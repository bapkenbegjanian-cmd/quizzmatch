Sample questions

  1. What does « pomme » mean?
    Apricot · Apple · Pineapple · Pear
    Show the answerApple : Une pomme par jour…
  2. How do you say "I'm hungry" in French?
    J'ai froid · J'ai faim · J'ai peur · J'ai sommeil
    Show the answerJ'ai faim : « J'ai froid » means I'm cold.
  3. What does « pas cher » mean?
    Cheap · Chic · Happy · Fast
    Show the answerCheap : The opposite is « cher ».
  4. What does « à plus tard » mean?
    I see you · See you later · Look at me · Good night
    Show the answerSee you later : Often shortened to « à plus ».
  5. What does « cuisine » mean?
    Kitchen · Bedroom · Living room · Garden
    Show the answerKitchen : It also means cooking.
  6. What does « déception » mean?
    Disappointment · Deception · Decision · Reception
    Show the answerDisappointment : False friend: "deception" is « tromperie ».
  7. What does « manger » mean?
    To eat · To manage · To hang · To walk
    Show the answerTo eat : « Bon appétit ! »
  8. What does « Quel âge as-tu ? » mean?
    How old are you? · How are you? · Where do you live? · What's your name?
    Show the answerHow old are you? : Answer: « J'ai 20 ans ».
  9. What does « laid » mean?
    Ugly · Kind · Weird · Sad
    Show the answerUgly : Milk is « lait ».
  10. What does « de rien » mean?
    You're welcome · Nothing to do · You're nice · Come in
    Show the answerYou're welcome : The answer to « merci ».
  11. What does « neveu » mean?
    Nephew · Niece · Cousin · Grandson
    Show the answerNephew : Niece is « nièce ».
  12. What does « un rhume » mean?
    A cold · A rum · A room · A rhyme
    Show the answerA cold : « J'ai un rhume » : I have a cold.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "French words" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "French words" quiz?

91 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
