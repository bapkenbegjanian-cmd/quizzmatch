Sample questions

  1. Which organ pumps blood around the body?
    The heart · The lungs · The liver · The stomach
    Show the answerThe heart : It beats about 100,000 times a day.
  2. What is the longest bone in the human body?
    The femur · The tibia · The humerus · The spine
    Show the answerThe femur : The thigh bone.
  3. What colour is the blood that comes out of a cut?
    Red · Blue · Purple · Green
    Show the answerRed : Blood is never blue, even in the veins.
  4. Which vitamin does our body make with sunlight?
    Vitamin D · Vitamin C · Vitamin A · Vitamin B12
    Show the answerVitamin D : It helps bones absorb calcium.
  5. How many bones are in an adult skeleton?
    206 · 106 · 306 · 186
    Show the answer206 : A baby has more; some fuse as we grow.
  6. Which parts of the blood help a cut heal?
    Platelets · Red blood cells · White blood cells · Hormones
    Show the answerPlatelets : They form a clot.
  7. Which is the biggest muscle in the body?
    The gluteus maximus · The biceps · The heart · The quadriceps
    Show the answerThe gluteus maximus : It helps us stand and climb stairs.
  8. How many chambers does the human heart have?
    4 · 2 · 3 · 6
    Show the answer4 : Two atria and two ventricles.
  9. Which sense is partly lost when your nose is blocked?
    Taste · Sight · Hearing · Touch
    Show the answerTaste : Smell makes up a big part of what we call taste.
  10. How many vertebrae are in the spine, not counting the sacrum and coccyx?
    24 · 33 · 12 · 18
    Show the answer24 : 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar.
  11. Which gland in the neck controls metabolism?
    The thyroid · The pituitary · The adrenals · The thymus
    Show the answerThe thyroid : It's shaped like a butterfly.
  12. Which part of the brain mainly handles balance and coordination?
    The cerebellum · The hippocampus · The frontal cortex · The hypothalamus
    Show the answerThe cerebellum : It sits at the back, beneath the brain.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "Human body" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "Human body" quiz?

36 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
