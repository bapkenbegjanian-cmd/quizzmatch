  1. How many rings are on the Olympic flag?
    5 · 4 · 6 · 7
    Show the answer5 : They stand for the union of the continents.
  2. Which Jamaican sprinter holds the 100 m world record?
    Usain Bolt · Carl Lewis · Yohan Blake · Asafa Powell
    Show the answerUsain Bolt : 9.58 seconds in 2009.
  3. Which American swimmer won 23 Olympic gold medals?
    Michael Phelps · Ryan Lochte · Mark Spitz · Caeleb Dressel
    Show the answerMichael Phelps : The most decorated athlete in Olympic history.
  4. How long is a marathon?
    42.195 km · 40 km · 21.1 km · 50 km
    Show the answer42.195 km : Half of it is a half-marathon.
  5. Who revived the modern Olympic Games?
    Pierre de Coubertin · Jules Rimet · Avery Brundage · Jacques Rogge
    Show the answerPierre de Coubertin : The first modern Games were held in Athens in 1896.
  6. In which river were some swimming events of Paris 2024 held?
    The Seine · The Loire · The Rhône · The Marne
    Show the answerThe Seine : Triathlon and open-water swimming.
  7. Which new sport made its Olympic debut at Paris 2024?
    Breaking · Skateboarding · Surfing · Climbing
    Show the answerBreaking : Skateboarding, surfing and climbing arrived in Tokyo.
  8. In which year did France host the Winter Games in Albertville?
    1992 · 1968 · 1988 · 2002
    Show the answer1992 : Grenoble had hosted the 1968 Games.
  9. Which country topped the gold medal table at Paris 2024?
    The United States · China · France · Japan
    Show the answerThe United States : Tied with China on 40 each, but with more medals overall.
  10. In which year did women first compete in the modern Games?
    1900 · 1896 · 1928 · 1948
    Show the answer1900 : In Paris, notably in tennis and golf.
  11. Which event combines cross-country skiing and rifle shooting?
    Biathlon · Nordic combined · Skeleton · Modern pentathlon
    Show the answerBiathlon : Martin Fourcade won 5 Olympic titles in it.
  12. Which long jump world record, set in 1991, still stands?
    8.95 m · 8.50 m · 9.20 m · 8.10 m
    Show the answer8.95 m : Set by Mike Powell in Tokyo.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "Olympic Games" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "Olympic Games" quiz?

35 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
