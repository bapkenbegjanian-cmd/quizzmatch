Sample questions

  1. Which organ pumps blood around the body?
    The liver · The heart · The lungs · The kidneys
    Show the answerThe heart : It beats about 100,000 times a day.
  2. Roughly how fast does light travel?
    300 km/s · 3,000 km/s · 300,000 km/s · 3 million km/s
    Show the answer300,000 km/s : It takes about 8 minutes to reach us from the Sun.
  3. What is the largest organ of the human body?
    The liver · The brain · The skin · The intestine
    Show the answerThe skin : It weighs several kilos in an adult.
  4. Which blood type is the universal red blood cell donor?
    A+ · AB+ · O negative · B negative
    Show the answerO negative : It can be given to almost anyone in an emergency.
  5. What is the chemical symbol for iron?
    Fe · Fr · Ir · F
    Show the answerFe : From the Latin "ferrum".
  6. What is the unit of electrical power?
    The watt · The volt · The ampere · The ohm
    Show the answerThe watt : The volt measures voltage, the ampere current.
  7. How many Nobel Prizes did Marie Curie win?
    2 · 1 · 3 · 0
    Show the answer2 : Physics in 1903, chemistry in 1911.
  8. Which organ filters blood to make urine?
    The kidneys · The liver · The stomach · The lungs
    Show the answerThe kidneys : We have two of them.
  9. What is the basic unit of every living thing?
    The cell · The atom · The organ · The muscle
    Show the answerThe cell : Our body has tens of trillions of them.
  10. What is the pH of pure water?
    7 · 0 · 14 · 1
    Show the answer7 : 7 is neutral: neither acidic nor basic.
  11. How many chambers does the human heart have?
    4 · 2 · 3 · 6
    Show the answer4 : Two atria and two ventricles.
  12. Which force keeps us on the ground?
    Gravity · Magnetism · Friction · Pressure
    Show the answerGravity : The same force keeps the Moon going around Earth.

Frequently asked questions

Is the "Science & the human body" quiz free?

Yes. Every QuizzMatch quiz is free, with no sign-up and no app to install. All you need is a browser, on your phone or computer.

How many questions are in the "Science & the human body" quiz?

92 questions across three levels (easy, medium, hard). Each game draws 10 at random, so no two games are the same.

How do you score points?

Every right answer scores points, with a bonus if you answer quickly. On hard, the timer is shorter but the bonus is bigger.

Can I play against friends?

Yes. At the end of a game, share the challenge link: your friends play exactly the same questions and see the score to beat. Duel mode also lets you play live, each on your own phone.
